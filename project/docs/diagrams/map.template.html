news-scape Operations Map: From News Source to Analyst

news-scape development team

Project news-scape, branch dev/us038, state after ADR-0026

Completed 2026-10-22 · Scope: processing flow, operations and change governance

Abstract

news-scape collects news from 8 Vietnamese financial sources, normalizes it into Silver records, analyzes each article with a language model and delivers the results to 5 analysts as a daily Excel file. This document describes the whole process on one page: six layers from L0 to L5, the data stores, 15 invariants and 26 architecture decisions. Data flows one way from Bronze to Silver, through the Article Lane and out to the Lakehouse, and every boundary has a JSON Schema contract and a validation gate. One wave processes 100 articles in two batches of 50 and completes only when coverage reaches at least 90% in both result tables. After the ADR-0026 consolidation the system has two autonomy levels (L0 and L1), a single publish path (publish.py calling publish_day()), one user manifest and one users/output directory tree. The Lakehouse currently holds 32,981 articles in 283 daily partitions, from 2025-10-01 to 2026-10-19. Five items remain open, one of which waits for the operator to move 607 Excel files to the new tree.

Keywords: news-scape; Article Lane; medallion architecture; Lakehouse; wave; ADR-0026; autonomous operations.
